Exploring is the reward

Four mechanics that turn every trip — commuting, coffee runs, evening walks — into a play session.

Fog of war

The map is covered like in Warcraft. Only places you have truly walked through are revealed — and stay revealed forever.

Check in for XP

The app knows which place you are near. One tap to check in, earn XP, and keep your daily streak alive.

Unlock districts

First time in District 3? The whole district lights up with a "New area discovered" banner — clear all 12 to finish the game.

Weekly quests

"Reveal 3 unknown sites", "Walk 5 km today" — small quests that keep you out on the street instead of on the couch.

Three steps to start

No account, no setup. Open the app and step outside.

1

Turn on location

NoPlace draws a sight radius around your position — like a unit in a strategy game.

2

Walk and lift the fog

Every alley you pass is recorded. Nearby places appear as "?" markers — visit them for rewards.

3

Climb the city ranks

Compare your explored percentage with friends and other players in your city on the leaderboard.
